      How to read this map

      Each box is a topic. Click one to open its card and light up everything it builds on (and everything that builds on it). Hover to see direct connections. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom.

      Flow
      Left to right is "what you need first." An arrow from A to B means B builds on A. If a later topic isn't clicking, follow its arrows back.
      Duality
      Electrostatics on the left, magnetostatics on the right, with each topic lined up against its twin. The vector-calculus tools and the one law that uses both fields sit in the middle.
      Units
      The same topics grouped the way the course is organized.
      Maxwell
      Just the four laws of static fields as a 2×2 grid — which field, and whether you ask how it spreads out or how it circulates — with the theorems that convert integral and differential forms, and what hangs off each law.

      Focus on this path hides everything not connected to the open topic. I know this lets you mark topics shaky / okay / solid; My gaps then shows only your shaky topics and what depends on them. Marks are saved in this browser only — nobody else sees them.

      Marks can be moved between browsers: Copy under the legend gives a short text code, Paste applies one.

      Keyboard: ← prerequisite · → next topic · ↑↓ neighbors in the same column · Esc closes.

      Dashed boxes are exits: where the material leads after this course. Dashed lines join the integral and differential forms of the same law.
